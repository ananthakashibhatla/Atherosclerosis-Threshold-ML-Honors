# Objectives — Iteration 01

## Objective 1 — Establish the Literature Foundation
Document why logistic growth, nonlinear threshold behavior, and data-driven plaque prediction are reasonable research ingredients.

## Objective 2 — Establish the Calculus Foundation
Derive equilibria and perform sign/stability analysis of the threshold-logistic ODE.

## Objective 3 — Build a Reproducible Synthetic Dataset
Generate multiple plaque trajectories while varying controlled mathematical parameters and later controlled measurement noise.

## Objective 4 — Build a Clean Data Pipeline
Inspect, clean, preprocess, and transform raw trajectory observations into a model-ready trajectory-level table.

## Objective 5 — Perform EDA
Understand distributions, correlations, class balance, trajectories, threshold distance, and noise behavior before fitting models.

## Objective 6 — Establish Linear-Regression Baselines
Move from a one-predictor linear model to multiple-predictor models and compare held-out performance.

## Objective 7 — Establish Logistic-Regression Baselines
Move from a one-predictor logistic classifier to multiple-predictor classifiers and compare held-out performance.

## Objective 8 — Interpret, Not Just Score
Analyze where models succeed or fail, especially close to `T` and under increased measurement noise.
